# Objection Library

Use these branches to reduce pressure and route appropriately. If someone opts
out, is angry, says wrong number, threatens legal action, or asks not to be
contacted, stop selling and suppress according to policy.

| Objection | Call Response | SMS Response | What Not To Say | When To Stop | Human Route | Compliance Note |
