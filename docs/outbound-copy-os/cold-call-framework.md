# Cold Call Framework

Cold calls should be short, transparent, and useful. The agent must disclose
identity, keep the reason concrete, ask permission where possible, and stop
cleanly when the lead opts out or becomes hostile. These are drafting templates,
not permission to call.

## Universal Call Shape

1. Identity: "Hi [first_name], this is [agent_name] with [brokerage]."
2. Pattern interrupt: "I know this is out of the blue, so I will be brief."
3. Permission: "Do you have 20 seconds for why I called?"
4. Reason: one sentence tied to audience and offer.
5. Probe: one diagnostic question.
6. Value: one useful next-step deliverable.
7. Close: low-pressure appointment or permission to send details.
8. Stop rule: if they ask not to be contacted, acknowledge and end.

## Standard Objection Branches

Apply these branches to every audience play above. Audience-specific objection
posture is simple: buyers get a shortlist/criteria reset, sellers get a
value/timing reset, expired listings get a recovery-plan reset, FSBO sellers get
a checklist/buyer-feedback reset, investors get a buy-box reset, relocation
buyers get an area-fit reset, and past leads get an active/paused/solved reset.

| Objection | Call Response |
| --- | --- |
| Not interested | "Totally fair. I will not push. Should I mark this as not relevant and avoid follow-up?" |
| Send me info | "I can. To avoid sending noise, should it be a shortlist, value check, or timing plan?" |
| How did you get my number? | "Fair question. It came from [approved source category]. If you do not want contact, I can mark that now." |
| Are you a realtor? | "Yes, I am [agent_name] with [brokerage]. My goal is to see if a practical real estate next step is useful, not pressure you." |
| I already have an agent | "Understood. I do not want to interfere. If you are represented, your agent should be the point person. I can mark that and step back." |

## Tone Rules

- Use short sentences.
- Ask one question at a time.
- Never argue with a no.
- Never imply guaranteed outcomes.
- Never mention sensitive inferred data.
- Route legal, angry, represented, or complex finance situations to a human.
