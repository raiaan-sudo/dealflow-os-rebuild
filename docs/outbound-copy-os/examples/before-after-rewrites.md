# Before And After Rewrites

## Buyer Hook

Before:

```text
Unlock exclusive properties in your dream neighborhood today.
```

After:

```text
Want a short list of [market] homes that fit your budget, area, and timing?
```

Why better: specific, low-friction, no fake exclusivity.

Compliance note: no secret-access implication.

## Seller Hook

Before:

```text
We have qualified buyers ready to pay top dollar for your home.
```

After:

```text
I can check current buyer demand for homes like yours and give you a realistic
value range.
```

Why better: useful without guaranteeing a buyer or price.

Compliance note: buyer-match claims require proof.

## Appointment Ask

Before:

```text
When are you free for a consultation?
```

After:

```text
Would a 10-minute value/timing check be useful this week?
```

Why better: clearer deliverable and lower commitment.

Compliance note: avoids pressure.

## Objection Reply

Before:

```text
You should at least hear me out.
```

After:

```text
Totally fair. I can close the loop here if it is not relevant.
```

Why better: respects negative intent and reduces complaints.

Compliance note: stop when requested.
