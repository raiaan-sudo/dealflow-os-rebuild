# Example: Buyer Cold Call

## Strong Copy

```text
Hi [first_name], this is [agent_name] with [brokerage]. I know this is out of
the blue, so I will be brief. I help buyers around [market] sort out which
homes actually fit their budget and timing. Are you still looking, or did that
pause?
```

If yes:

```text
Got it. What matters most right now: area, monthly payment, or timing?
```

Close:

```text
I can build a short list around that and walk you through it in 10 minutes. Is
today or tomorrow better?
```

## Bad Copy

```text
I have exclusive homes you need to see before everyone else. You do not want to
miss this market.
```

## Why Bad Copy Fails

- Implies secret/private access.
- Uses pressure and fake urgency.
- Does not ask a diagnostic question.
- Sounds generic and salesy.

## Improved Rewrite

```text
I can build a shortlist from active and permitted coming-soon options that fit
your budget, area, and timing. Want me to send the short version first?
```

## Compliance Notes

Use only approved source context. Do not claim early access unless permitted by
law, MLS/brokerage rules, and campaign review.
