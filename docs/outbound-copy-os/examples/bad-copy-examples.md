# Bad Copy Examples

## Fake Urgency

Bad:

```text
Last chance to buy before prices skyrocket.
```

Why it fails: unsupported prediction and pressure.

Rewrite:

```text
If buying this year is still possible, I can help you compare what fits today's
payment range.
```

Compliance note: no price/rate predictions without approved support.

## Misleading Private Access

Bad:

```text
I can get you secret listings before the public.
```

Why it fails: implies unlawful insider access.

Rewrite:

```text
I can include active and permitted coming-soon options that match your criteria.
```

Compliance note: respect MLS, brokerage, and fair-housing rules.

## Protected-Class Steering

Bad:

```text
This is a perfect neighborhood for young families.
```

Why it fails: familial-status implication.

Rewrite:

```text
This area has three-bedroom homes near parks, transit, and daily amenities.
```

Compliance note: describe property and amenities, not protected people.

## Sensitive Data Personalization

Bad:

```text
Since you may have a lot of equity and be ready to downsize, I wanted to call.
```

Why it fails: creepy inference and possible age/financial sensitivity.

Rewrite:

```text
I had a quick homeowner question for [market]. Is moving this year on the radar,
or is it a firm hold?
```

Compliance note: do not use private/sensitive data unless approved.
