# Example: FSBO

## Strong Copy

```text
Hi [first_name], this is [agent_name] with [brokerage]. I saw the home is being
sold by owner. I know you may not want an agent. Are you open to buyer feedback
or a FSBO checklist, or would you rather I leave it alone?
```

## Bad Copy

```text
FSBO homes never sell for what they should. You need a real agent.
```

## Why Bad Copy Fails

- Insults the seller's decision.
- Unsupported claim.
- Creates resistance.

## Improved Rewrite

```text
You can stay FSBO and still pressure-test pricing, showings, buyer financing,
and paperwork. Want the checklist?
```

## Compliance Notes

Do not give legal advice. Route contract, disclosure, commission, and paperwork
questions to a licensed professional or attorney as appropriate.
