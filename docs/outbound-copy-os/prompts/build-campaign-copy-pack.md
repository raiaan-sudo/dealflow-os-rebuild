## Safety Rules

- Draft only; no live campaign creation.
- No SMS/calls/forms/provider generation.
- No sensitive data or credential values.
- Compliance guardrails and scoring rubric are mandatory.

1. Campaign strategy.
2. Cold call script.
3. SMS sequence.
4. Voicemail.
5. Follow-up path.
6. Objection branches.
7. Funnel hook.
8. A/B variants.
9. Compliance notes.
10. Scorecard.
11. Owner review checklist.

## Scoring Requirements

Score each asset and the pack overall. Compliance safety must be 10 for every
asset.

diagnostic review.
