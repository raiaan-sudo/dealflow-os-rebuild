# Voicemail And Follow-Up Framework

Voicemail should create recognition and permission for a single clean follow-up.
It should not pressure, hide identity, imply urgency, or contain sensitive lead
data.

## Voicemail Formula

```text
Hi [first_name], this is [agent_name] with [brokerage].
I had a quick [buying/selling/property] question for [market].
No pressure, but if [offer] would be useful, call or text me back at [callback_number].
Again, [agent_name] with [brokerage], [callback_number].
```

## Audience Voicemails

| Audience | Voicemail |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer | "Hi [first_name], [agent_name] with [brokerage]. I had a quick question about whether [market] is still on your home search list. If a tighter shortlist would help, call or text me at [callback_number]." |
| Seller | "Hi [first_name], [agent_name] with [brokerage]. I had a quick [market] value/timing question. If a simple home-value check would be useful, call or text me at [callback_number]." |
| Expired | "Hi [first_name], [agent_name]. I had one practical thought on why the listing may have stalled. If you are still considering selling, call or text me at [callback_number]." |
| FSBO | "Hi [first_name], [agent_name] with [brokerage]. I had a quick question about the FSBO in [market] and whether buyer feedback would be useful. My number is [callback_number]." |
| Reactivation | "Hi [first_name], [agent_name]. We spoke before about [buying/selling] in [market]. I am checking whether that is active, paused, or solved. Call or text me at [callback_number]." |

## Follow-Up Cadence

Use only if consent, DNC, suppression, channel, and local law allow follow-up.

| Touch | Purpose | Copy Direction | Stop Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Initial relevance check | Identify, ask one question, include opt-out where required. | Opt-out, wrong number, anger, represented conflict. |
| 2 | Value clarification | Offer one deliverable: shortlist, value check, timeline plan. | Any negative response. |
| 3 | Choice close | "Useful or not relevant?" | No reply after final touch or any opt-out. |
| 4 | Reactivation later | Only with approved basis and elapsed time policy. | Unclear consent or stale source. |

## Callback Script

```text
Thanks for calling back. I will keep it simple.
I reached out because [reason tied to offer].
Before I send anything or suggest a next step, what is actually going on with
[buying/selling/property] right now?
```

## Follow-Up QA

- Is the sender identity clear?
- Is the reason for contact truthful?
- Is there one CTA?
- Is opt-out included where required?
- Does the follow-up stop after negative intent?
- Does the copy avoid claims about protected classes, income, credit, urgency,
  or guaranteed outcomes?
